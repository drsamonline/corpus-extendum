## The licence in one line

Everything in this repository — catalogue, metadata, typesetting, site, scripts —
is released under **CC0 1.0**, a public-domain dedication. The books themselves
are public domain works; each record states its own basis.

## What is deliberately absent

- **No TERMS.md.** There is no account, no purchase, no hosted service and
  nothing to agree to. A terms document would invent an agreement that does not
  exist.
- **No EULA.** No binary is distributed.
- **No DISCLAIMER.md for advice.** The library gives no medical, legal or
  financial advice. It also gives no AI-generated content.

## Status

These documents describe the software as shipped and are updated in the same
commit as any change to how it handles data or whose work it contains. They are
not legal advice. The rights claims about individual books are the part most
worth a professional's eye — see [docs/RIGHTS.md](docs/RIGHTS.md), which records
the basis for each one so it can be checked rather than trusted.
