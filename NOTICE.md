# Attribution and notices

Every book here is a public-domain work. The **edition** used to produce the
files is named in the record, and credit stays there permanently.

## Standard Ebooks

Nine of the ten seed books are based on Standard Ebooks editions
(https://standardebooks.org). Their contributions to those editions — the
proofreading, the typesetting, the artwork — are released by them under the
**CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication**, which is why they can be reused
here without conditions.

The PDFs in this library are **ours**, not theirs: they are typeset from their
source XHTML with our own stylesheet, at A5, with our own title page. If you
want the edition as Standard Ebooks intended it, download the EPUB — that is
their file, unmodified apart from image downscaling.

## Project Gutenberg

One seed book (The Song Celestial) comes from the GITenberg mirror of Project
Gutenberg text. Project Gutenberg's texts are public domain; the licence they
attach is a separate agreement, whose terms we respect:

- attribution to the source ebook number is kept in the record;
- redistribution here is free of charge;
- no Project Gutenberg name, logo or trademark is used, and nothing here
  suggests Project Gutenberg endorses this library.

## Internet Archive

Reserved for a scanned edition that exists nowhere else. The item's own rights
statement is checked per item before it is shelved.

## What is ours

Catalogue records, metadata, typesetting, generated covers, the site and the
pipeline: CC0 1.0. Take them.

## Corrections

If a rights basis here is wrong, open an issue. The fix is one commit to a
record and one line removed from a vault's `index.txt`; the book disappears
from the catalogue on the next rebuild.
