Gujarati religious texts on the Qur'an, salat, zakat and Karbala, published here by the site owner. These are not public domain and they are not part of the library. They are shared for reading, with the authorship of each one stated.

On rights

The texts attributed to Allama Ghulam Ahmad Parwez (d. 1985) remain the property of their rights holders. The Gujarati translations by Dr Sohil Momin are his own work; the original English texts they translate remain the property of their authors.

Two of these — Criminals of Islam and Karbala: Satya ke Kalpana — translate Dr. Shabbir Ahmed, M.D. (1947–2022). He died in November 2022, so his rights now sit with his estate, and his work is protected in India until 2082. Publishing a translation of it needs that estate's permission, not his.

Nothing here is offered as public domain, and none of it is claimed as free to redistribute by anyone else. If you hold rights in any item and want it removed, open an issue on the repository and it will be taken down.
